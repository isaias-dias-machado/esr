All files include an AAC-LC audio track, mono, 48 kHz.
The audio is synthetic and was generated specifically for TP1: two short reference tones per second (880 Hz and 440 Hz). The track can be used to verify playback continuity without introducing third-party audiovisual content.

The values 500k, 1000k, and 2000k in the DASH representation filenames refer to the approximate bitrate of the VIDEO component. The audio track adds a small additional bitrate to the total stream.

Content Source

The videos were generated specifically for TP1 using FFmpeg's testsrc2 source.
The three DASH representations were produced from the same source and share the same timeline.
The audio was also generated using FFmpeg synthetic filters.
No third-party audiovisual content is included.

Note for Stage 3

The video_rtp.mp4 file contains audio, but the RTP commands in the lab guide use -an and transmit only the video component, keeping the RTP/RTCP experiment focused and simple. The audio remains available for local playback and inspection using ffplay/ffprobe.
